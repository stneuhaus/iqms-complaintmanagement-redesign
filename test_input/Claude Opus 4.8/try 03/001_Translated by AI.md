# In case of injection initiation failure

## EYLEA® Replacement Program

In the case of an injection failure only and after evaluation of the request, the EYLEA® replacement program may provide healthcare professionals with a replacement EYLEA® unit.

The case will be reviewed based on the consistency of the information provided in this form.

Form to be returned completed and signed by email, fax, or mail to:
- Email: programme-remplacement-eylea@universalmedica.com
- Fax: 01 86 95 44 85
- Address: Universal Medica, 106 bureaux de la colline, 92213 SAINT CLOUD cedex

To speed up the processing of your request, please prefer sending by email.

**IMPORTANT:** The information, duly completed, must be consistent in order to allow the admissibility of the request to be assessed. Bayer HealthCare reserves the right to approve or refuse a request.

**This form is not intended for the reporting of adverse events.** To report an adverse event, please report it via https://signalement.social-sante.gouv.fr. You can also contact Bayer at the following number for Pharmacovigilance and medical information: 0 800 87 54 54, or use our reporting page https://safetrack-public.bayer.com/
