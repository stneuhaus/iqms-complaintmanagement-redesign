# In the event of a failed injection initiation

## EYLEA® Replacement Program

In the case of a failed injection only and after evaluation of the request, the EYLEA® replacement program may make an EYLEA® replacement unit available to healthcare professionals.

The case will be studied depending on the consistency of the information provided in this form.

Form to be returned completed and signed by email, fax, or mail to:

- Email: programme-remplacement-eylea@universalmedica.com
- Fax: 01 86 95 44 85
- Address: Universal Medica, 106 bureaux de la colline, 92213 SAINT CLOUD cedex

To speed up the processing of your request, please favor sending by email.

**IMPORTANT:** The information, duly completed, must be consistent in order to allow the admissibility of the request to be assessed. Bayer HealthCare reserves the right to approve or refuse a request.

**This form is not intended for the reporting of adverse events.**
To report an adverse event, please report it via https://signalement.social-sante.gouv.fr. You may also contact Bayer at the following number for Pharmacovigilance and medical information: 0 800 87 54 54 or use our reporting page https://safetrack-public.bayer.com/
