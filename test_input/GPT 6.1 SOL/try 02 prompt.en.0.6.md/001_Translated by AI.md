# In the event of failure to initiate an injection
## EYLEA® Replacement Program

**Only in the event of an unsuccessful injection and after assessment of the request**, the EYLEA® Replacement Program may provide healthcare professionals with one replacement unit of EYLEA®.

The case will be reviewed based on the consistency of the information provided in this form.

Return the completed and signed form by email, fax, or mail to:
